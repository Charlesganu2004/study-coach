Model and access

Groq GPT OSS 120B was selected in the project evaluation. Changing the model can change response quality and speed.

Get a Groq key ↗ · Select a free account. Leave blank to keep your saved key.

Get an NVIDIA key ↗ · Look for Free Endpoint access.

A few things to know.

Free access has limits

Groq and NVIDIA provide free access subject to account eligibility and quotas. Check your provider console. This app does not enable billing, switch to paid endpoints, or guarantee free access forever.

Where your keys live

In a private .env file in this app’s data folder.

This is a local credential file, not an encrypted vault. You can edit it after closing the app. Never share it.

What leaves this computer

When you ask, your question and saved source text go to your selected provider. Notes and history otherwise stay here. Do not submit private material unless you are comfortable with that provider’s terms.

Need to study without a key?

You can read and organize your notes here without a key. Cloud answers need your own account. No keyless cloud service is connected.
